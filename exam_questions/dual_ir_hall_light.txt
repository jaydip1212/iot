QUESTION 20 - TWO-SENSOR HALLWAY LIGHT
======================================

Problem
Use two IR sensors at opposite ends of a hallway. Turn a light on while either end detects a person, then keep it on for 20 seconds after both sensors are clear.

Components and pins
Arduino Uno; IR sensor A OUT D2 and sensor B OUT D4 (LOW = detected); lamp LED D13; Serial Monitor.

Tasks (10 marks each)
1. Read and report both IR sensors.
2. Turn on the lamp when either sensor detects a person.
3. After both become clear, hold the lamp on for 20 seconds; a new detection restarts the timer.

ANSWER
The two sensors are combined with OR logic. The timer begins only after both sensors are clear.

const int sensorA=2, sensorB=4, lamp=13;
bool holdActive=false;
unsigned long clearSince=0, lastPrint=0;
void setup(){
  pinMode(sensorA,INPUT); pinMode(sensorB,INPUT); pinMode(lamp,OUTPUT);
  Serial.begin(9600);
}
void loop(){
  unsigned long now=millis();
  bool a=digitalRead(sensorA)==LOW;
  bool b=digitalRead(sensorB)==LOW;
  bool occupied=a||b;
  if(occupied) holdActive=false;
  else if(!holdActive){ clearSince=now; holdActive=true; }
  bool lampOn=occupied || (holdActive && now-clearSince<20000UL);
  digitalWrite(lamp,lampOn?HIGH:LOW);
  if(now-lastPrint>=300){
    lastPrint=now;
    Serial.print("A="); Serial.print(a?"Person":"Clear");
    Serial.print(" B="); Serial.print(b?"Person":"Clear");
    Serial.print(" Lamp="); Serial.println(lampOn?"ON":"OFF");
  }
}
